Filament Flow Calibration

Do bed leveling and a first-layer Z offset first. Calibrate e-steps / rotation distance so 100 mm requested is 100 mm extruded. Pressure advance should already be in the ballpark.

What this test is

This is the method the community slicers settled on in 2025–2026:

  • OrcaSlicer recommended: one pass of chips around your current extrusion multiplier (YOLO), range −0.05 to +0.05 in steps of 0.01. New multiplier = old ± the chip you pick. Top surface is Archimedean chords so gaps and a raised inner ring are obvious.
  • Ellis' Print Tuning Guide: judge the top surface, not a caliper on one wall. Use a real-sized chip with sparse infill so first-layer squish does not print through to the top. Turn ironing off. If two chips look equal, pick the higher multiplier.

The old SuperSlicer five-cube test used 100% solid 10 mm cubes. Ellis calls that out specifically: first-layer squish carries to the top and the patch is too small to read.

How to run it

  1. Select the filament you are calibrating. The chips are offset from that filament's current extrusion multiplier.
  2. Click Generate recommended. You get eleven 22×22×3 mm chips. Each has a raised number 1–11 on the top front of the chip. That number matches the buttons in this window. Chip 1 is −0.05, chip 6 is 0, chip 11 is +0.05.
  3. Slice and print. Each object is printed separately so nozzle pressure can settle.
  4. Inspect the top of each chip, looking at the centre:
    • Best: smoothest top, no valleys between the spiral/arcs, no raised ridge at the inner spiral.
    • Too low: gaps or shadows between lines.
    • Too high: rough, shiny pile-up, or a raised ring where the inner spiral meets the outer arcs.
  5. Re-open this window. Click the same number you read off the printed tab. SuperSlicer does the math and shows current + modifier = new. Click Apply to filament, then save the filament preset.
  6. Optional: click Generate fine for a ±0.025 pass in 0.005 steps around the new value, then pick a tile again.

Formula

new_multiplier = old_multiplier + chip_modifier

The chip name is the modifier, not the absolute multiplier. Do not multiply by (100 + percent) / 100 unless you are reading an old 2-pass guide.

Notes

  • Do not use wall-thickness calipers for this setting. That measures wobble and line width more than flow, and a 0.02 mm error is a 5% multiplier change.
  • If dimensions are still off after a good top, use shrinkage / horizontal expansion. Do not chase that with flow.
  • This test sets complete-objects and turns off layer-time slowdown. Reset those if you do not want them left dirty on the print/filament presets.
