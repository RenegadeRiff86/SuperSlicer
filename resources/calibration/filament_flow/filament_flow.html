Filament Flow Calibration

Do bed leveling and a first-layer Z offset first. Calibrate e-steps / rotation distance so 100 mm requested is 100 mm extruded. Pressure advance should already be in the ballpark.

What this test is

This uses OrcaSlicer's official YOLO LinearFlow models and SuperSlicer's filament extrusion-multiplier math:

  • Generate recommended loads Orca's Orca-LinearFlow.3mf: eleven chips around your current extrusion multiplier, range −0.05 to +0.05 in steps of 0.01. New multiplier = old ± the chip you pick.
  • Generate fine loads Orca's Orca-LinearFlow_fine.3mf (perfectionist pass, 0.005 steps).
  • The top of each square is the evaluation surface (Archimedean chords). The number is on a connected tab, not on the top, so you can tell the chips apart without ruining the surface you judge.
  • The official model is 2 mm tall and drawn for a 0.4 mm nozzle. SuperSlicer computes the plate scale from the nozzle, first-layer height, first-layer line width, and layer height currently selected. Those print settings are not overwritten.
  • Ellis' Print Tuning Guide: judge the top, not a caliper on one wall. Sparse infill, ironing off. If two chips look equal, pick the higher multiplier.

How to run it

  1. Select the filament you are calibrating. The chips are offset from that filament's current extrusion multiplier.
  2. Click Generate recommended. SuperSlicer loads Orca's arranged plate. Each chip has a tab engraved -.05 … 0 … .05.
  3. Slice and print. All chips share one plate. Do not use sequential printing.
  4. Inspect the top of each square, looking at the centre:
    • Best: smoothest top, no valleys between the spiral/arcs, no raised ridge at the inner spiral.
    • Too low: gaps or shadows between lines.
    • Too high: rough, shiny pile-up, or a raised ring where the inner spiral meets the outer arcs.
  5. Re-open this window. Click the tile that matches the number on the printed tab. SuperSlicer does the math and shows current + modifier = new. Click Apply to filament, then save the filament preset.
  6. Optional: click Generate fine for the tighter Orca pass around the new value, then pick a tile again.

Formula

new_multiplier = old_multiplier + chip_modifier

That is Orca YOLO / Ellis linear math. The tab is the modifier, not the absolute multiplier. Do not multiply by (100 + percent) / 100 unless you are reading an old 2-pass guide.

Notes

  • Do not use wall-thickness calipers for this setting. That measures wobble and line width more than flow, and a 0.02 mm error is a 5% multiplier change.
  • If dimensions are still off after a good top, use shrinkage / horizontal expansion. Do not chase that with flow.
  • This test turns off sequential printing (complete objects) so the official plate fits. Reset that if you do not want it left dirty on the print preset.
  • Models come from OrcaSlicer (AGPLv3), resources/calib/filament_flow/.
